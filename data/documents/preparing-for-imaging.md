# Preparing for X-ray, Ultrasound, CT, and MRI

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

This page gives general administrative guidance. **Your ordering doctor's instructions take priority** over this page.

Radiology is in **Building B, Floor 1**. Phone: **(503) 555-0136**. All imaging requires an order from a doctor.

## X-ray

No special preparation is needed. You will be asked to remove jewelry and metal items from the area being imaged.

Appointment length: 15 minutes.

## Ultrasound

- **Abdominal ultrasound:** Do not eat or drink for **6 hours** before the scan.
- **Pelvic ultrasound:** Drink **1 liter of water** 1 hour before the scan, and do not empty your bladder before the scan.

Appointment length: 30 minutes.

## CT scan

If your scan uses contrast dye, do not eat for **4 hours** before the scan. Tell staff about any allergies or kidney problems.

The ordering doctor's office requests prior authorization from your insurance plan.

Appointment length: 30 minutes.

## MRI

- Remove all metal before the scan.
- Tell staff **before the scan** if you have a pacemaker, metal implants, or might be pregnant.
- The scan itself takes about **45 minutes**.

The ordering doctor's office requests prior authorization from your insurance plan.

Appointment length: 60 minutes.
