# How to Book an Appointment

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

## Ways to book

You can book an appointment in three ways:

- Call the appointments line at **(503) 555-0110** during outpatient hours.
- Call the department directly. Numbers are listed in the Departments and Doctors Directory.
- Use **CareVoice**, the hospital's virtual assistant.

## What you need to book

Please have your **patient ID** and **date of birth** ready. We use them to confirm your identity before booking, changing, or sharing details of any appointment.

## How far ahead you can book

Appointments can be booked up to **90 days** in advance.

Same-day appointments are available in **Family Medicine** and **Pediatrics** when there is an opening. Other departments do not offer same-day appointments.

## Booking limit

Each patient may have up to **3** upcoming appointments booked at one time.

## Referrals

Some departments need a referral before your first visit:

- **Cardiology:** new patients need a referral from a primary care doctor.
- **Radiology:** all imaging requires an order from a doctor.

Summit Care HMO members need a referral from their primary care doctor before **any** specialist visit.

## Doctors not accepting new patients

Dr. Samuel Brooks (Family Medicine) and Dr. Hannah Lindqvist (Dermatology) are not currently accepting new patients. Their existing patients can still book follow-up appointments. New patients can book with another doctor in the same department.

## Telehealth appointments

Video appointments are available in **Family Medicine**, **Cardiology**, and **Pediatrics**. Telehealth is not suitable for first visits that need a physical examination. See "Preparing for Your Visit" for how telehealth works.

## Interpreters

Free medical interpreters are available. Please ask for one when you book.
