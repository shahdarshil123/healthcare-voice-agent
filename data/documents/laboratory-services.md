# Laboratory Services and Blood Tests

## Location

The laboratory is in **Building A, Floor 1**.

## Hours

The laboratory accepts walk-in patients. No appointment is needed.

- **Monday to Friday:** 07:00–17:00
- **Saturday:** 08:00–12:00
- **Sunday:** Closed

## What you need

You need a **lab order from a doctor**. Please also bring your photo ID and insurance card.

## Fasting before a blood test

Some blood tests usually require fasting, including:

- Fasting glucose
- Lipid panel (cholesterol)

If your test requires fasting, do not eat or drink anything except **water** for **8 to 12 hours** before your test. Your doctor will tell you whether your test requires fasting.

## Getting your results

Results are usually available within **2 business days**. Your doctor's office will contact you with your results.
