# Preparing for Your Visit

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

## What to bring

Please bring the following to every appointment:

- Photo ID
- Insurance card
- A list of your current medications
- Your referral letter, if your department requires one

## When to arrive

New patients should arrive **20 minutes early** to complete registration.

If you arrive more than **15 minutes late**, we may need to reschedule your appointment.

## Children's appointments

A parent or guardian must attend with patients under 18.

## Telehealth (video) appointments

Telehealth is available in Family Medicine, Cardiology, and Pediatrics.

- A video link is sent to you by email **1 hour before** your appointment.
- Please test your camera, microphone, and connection **10 minutes before** your appointment.
- Telehealth is not suitable for first visits that need a physical examination.

## Interpreter services

Free medical interpreters are available. Please ask for one when you book your appointment.

## Accessibility

All buildings have step-free access. Wheelchairs are available at every main entrance.

## Parking

The visitor garage is on Lantern Way. The first 30 minutes are free, then $2 per hour, up to a maximum of $12 per day.
