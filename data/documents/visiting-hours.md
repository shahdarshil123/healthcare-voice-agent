# Visiting Hours and Visitor Guidelines

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

## General wards

Visiting hours on general wards are **09:00 to 20:00**, every day.

## Pediatrics ward

Parents and guardians may visit the pediatrics ward **at any time**, day or night.

Other visitors may visit the pediatrics ward from **10:00 to 19:00**.

## Number of visitors

Each patient may have up to **2 visitors** at a time.

## Visitor facilities

- **Parking:** Visitor garage on Lantern Way. The first 30 minutes are free, then $2 per hour, up to a maximum of $12 per day.
- **Cafeteria:** Building A, Floor 1. Open daily 07:00–19:00.
- **Wi-Fi:** Free guest network "Brightwater-Guest".
- **Accessibility:** All buildings have step-free access. Wheelchairs are available at every main entrance.

For questions about visiting, call the main hospital line at **(503) 555-0100**.
