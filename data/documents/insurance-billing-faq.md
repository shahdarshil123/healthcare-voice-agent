# Insurance and Billing FAQ

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

## Which insurance plans do you accept?

We accept the following plans:

- Evergreen Health Plan
- Cascade Mutual PPO
- Summit Care HMO
- Medicare
- Medicaid

If your plan is not listed, please call the billing office at **(503) 555-0120** before your visit.

## I don't have insurance. Can I still be seen?

Yes. Patients paying for their own care (self-pay) receive a **20% discount**.

## When do I pay my copay?

Your copay is collected at **check-in**.

## How can I pay?

We accept credit or debit cards, cash, and monthly payment plans.

## Can I set up a payment plan?

Yes. Monthly payment plans are available for balances of **$200 or more**. Contact the billing office to set one up.

## Do you offer financial assistance?

Yes. Financial assistance is available for eligible patients. Apply through the billing office at **(503) 555-0120**.

## Do I need prior authorization for a scan?

For **CT and MRI** scans, the ordering doctor's office requests prior authorization from your insurance plan. You do not need to request it yourself.

## Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

It depends on your plan and the department:

- **Summit Care HMO** members need a referral from their primary care doctor before **any** specialist visit.
- **Cardiology** requires a referral for all new patients, whatever their plan.
- **Radiology** requires an order from a doctor for all imaging.

## Can I get an itemized bill?

Yes. An itemized bill is available on request from the billing office.

## Are there fees for missed appointments?

Yes. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours' notice) cost $25 and no-shows cost $50. See the Appointment Cancellation, Rescheduling, and No-Show Policy for details and exceptions.
