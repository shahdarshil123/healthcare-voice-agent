# Hospital Information, Hours, and Contacts

## Emergencies

**If you have a medical emergency, call 911.** The Emergency Department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Do not use the appointments line or the CareVoice assistant for emergencies.

## CareVoice virtual assistant

CareVoice is the hospital's virtual assistant. It can help you find departments and doctors, check availability, book and cancel appointments, and answer questions about hospital policies.

CareVoice **cannot** give medical advice, diagnose conditions, or recommend treatments. For medical questions, please speak to your doctor.

## Other services

- **Parking:** Visitor garage on Lantern Way. First 30 minutes free, then $2 per hour, maximum $12 per day.
- **Cafeteria:** Building A, Floor 1. Open daily 07:00–19:00.
- **Wi-Fi:** Free guest network "Brightwater-Guest".
- **Lost and found:** Main reception, Building A.
- **Interpreters:** Free medical interpreters are available on request.
