# Appointment Cancellation, Rescheduling, and No-Show Policy

*Brightwater Community Hospital is fictional. This document is synthetic data for software development.*

At Brightwater Community Hospital, every missed appointment is time another patient could have used. This policy explains how to cancel or reschedule, and what happens if an appointment is missed.

## How much notice we need

Please cancel or reschedule **at least 24 hours before** your appointment time.

## How to cancel or reschedule

- Call the appointments line at **(503) 555-0110** during outpatient hours (Monday to Friday 08:00–18:00, Saturday 09:00–13:00).
- Or call the department directly. Department phone numbers are listed in the Departments Directory.

Please have your patient ID and appointment date ready.

## Rescheduling

Rescheduling with at least 24 hours' notice is free. Rescheduling with less than 24 hours' notice is treated the same as a late cancellation.

## Fees

| Situation | Fee |
|---|---|
| Cancelled or rescheduled 24 hours or more before the appointment | No fee |
| Cancelled or rescheduled less than 24 hours before the appointment (late cancellation) | $25 |
| Did not attend and did not cancel (no-show) | $50 |

Questions about a fee can be directed to the billing office at **(503) 555-0120**.

## When fees are waived

We waive late cancellation and no-show fees in the following situations:

- A medical emergency
- An admission to hospital
- A hospital closure due to severe weather

## Arriving late

If you arrive more than **15 minutes** after your appointment time, we may need to reschedule you so that other patients are seen on time.

New patients should arrive **20 minutes early** to complete registration.

## Booking limit

Each patient may have up to **3** upcoming appointments booked at one time.
